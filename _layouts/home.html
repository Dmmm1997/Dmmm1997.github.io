I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Automation at Southeast University, advised by Prof. Wankou Yang. My research focuses on multimodal large language models and agentic reinforcement learning, with applications in visual grounding, referring image and video segmentation, and multimodal agentic search.

I have gained research and algorithm internship experience at Ant Group (2026), Baidu (2024–2026), NIO (2022–2023), and ByteDance (2022). During my internship at Baidu, I was fortunate to work under the guidance of Dr. Jingdong Wang on multimodal models and video understanding. I have authored or co-authored 20+ peer-reviewed papers, including 13 first- or co-first-authored papers in leading AI conferences and journals such as TPAMI, TIP, ICML, ICCV, CVPR, and NeurIPS. My work has received 1,300+ Google Scholar citations, with two first-authored journal papers recognized as highly cited papers.
